# Bundled noto-cjk fonts

Unmodified Noto fonts based on Adobe's openly licensed CJK typefaces, licensed
under SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see `OFL.txt`, also embedded in About SoundCraft).
The font files retain their embedded copyright and trademark notices; these are
also reproduced in `COPYRIGHT.txt` and About SoundCraft.

Upstream: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/tree/f8d157532fbfaeda587e826d4cd5b21a49186f7c

The regular and bold pan-CJK collections include Japanese, Korean, Simplified
Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Hong Kong faces. SoundCraft uses collection
index 2 (Simplified Chinese) for shared Han characters; kana and Hangul are also
covered. Region-specific Han glyph variants are not selected automatically.
